------------------------------------------------------------
CORE CONCEPT
------------------------------------------------------------

Selah follows the same core philosophy and technology as
ZYRA while being designed specifically for the Christian
community.

Core principles (identical to ZYRA):
  • Serious relationships only
  • Marriage-focused
  • Family-first
  • AI-assisted compatibility
  • Safety and verification
  • Guided relationship journey
  • Trusted person involved throughout the journey

The technology, workflow and feature set remain the same
as ZYRA. Only the faith-specific branding, terminology
and content change.

------------------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT NOTE
------------------------------------------------------------

Selah is NOT a replacement or rebrand of ZYRA.
Selah is a completely separate application built
specifically for the Christian community.

ZYRA remains the dedicated Muslim marriage platform.

Both apps share the same core technology, AI systems,
safety features and overall philosophy, but each is
designed for its respective faith community using its
own branding, terminology, onboarding, educational
content and relationship guidance.

They operate as two independent products under the
same platform ecosystem.

------------------------------------------------------------
WALI EQUIVALENT — MY SHEPHERD
------------------------------------------------------------

The Wali system does NOT change functionally.
Functionality remains identical.
Only the wording and presentation change to fit a
Christian audience.

  Muslim App:    My Wali
  Christian App: My Shepherd

MY SHEPHERD

Purpose:
  A trusted Christian who walks alongside the user
  throughout their journey to finding a spouse and
  preparing for marriage.

Possible Shepherds include:
  • Parent
  • Pastor
  • Mentor
  • Church Elder
  • Mature Christian
  • Trusted family member

The Shepherd's purpose is to:
  • Guide
  • Encourage
  • Pray
  • Give wisdom
  • Support healthy decision-making
  • Walk alongside the relationship journey

The feature is NOT presented as replacing Jesus.
"Shepherd" refers to a trusted person providing guidance
throughout the user's journey to marriage.

The Shepherd can:
  • Accept invitation
  • View permitted relationship progress
  • Receive milestone updates
  • Encourage the couple
  • Offer advice
  • Pray for the couple
  • Help guide important decisions
  • Stay involved from the beginning through marriage

Permissions remain user-controlled.
