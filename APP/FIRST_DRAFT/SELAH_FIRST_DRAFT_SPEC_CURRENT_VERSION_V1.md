# Selah first phone draft — product specification

Status: Founder-requested first draft; presentation for private testing.
Authority: direct Founder instructions, 2026-10-13; not a production launch.

## Intended outcome

Create a separately installed Christian marriage app using the existing
request-based product foundation and familiar current visual treatment.
Iterate with the Founder after the first phone draft. Keep the source product
untouched and do not connect Selah to its accounts or live member data.

## First-draft content and routes

- Browse: local stock profiles and photo carousel, bounded Back/Next history,
  Journey invitation/cancel state, local filtering/hide/block, compatibility
  reveal and local code lookup presentation.
- Journey: up to three local Journeys, preparation and mutual confirmations,
  first greetings, compatibility reveal, stages, options, End and history.
- My Selah: requests, introductions, addressed updates and ended history.
- My Shepherd/Shepherd: trusted parent, pastor, mentor, church elder, mature
  Christian or family support; controlled local viewing/chat examples.
- Shepherd Circle: shared chat presentation with a discussion marker for a
  selected Journey, preserving the single conversation.
- Profile: existing local profile/photo/compatibility editors and settings.
- Private Home control: Seeker/Shepherd walkthroughs and retained welcome/
  onboarding presentation. Default startup remains the current Browse shell.

Retain the existing purple/gold, Poppins/Syne, card, navigation and motion
foundation for this first draft. (Superseded 3 Nov 2026, AD-AIOS-123: SELAH's
own look and colours are now being designed; the new look applies once LOCKed.) Own Selah wordmark and icon identify the app.
Adapt greetings and Christian questions semantically. No invented Bible
quotation or relabelled teaching from ZYRA's faith community is permitted.

## Explicitly not established

The private sample request, chat, credits, compatibility result, report and
membership presentations are not real member, identity-verification, safety
report, payment or scoring systems. No production database/API/provider,
company registration, product pricing ratification, account admission or
public asset licence clearance is claimed. These remain tracked below.

## Acceptance

Selah installs beside ZYRA without replacing it; opens unplugged in PROFILE;
all five main routes are usable; private role/onboarding controls are reachable;
Christian wording is present; no other-company accounts/data/network are
accessed. Physical appearance and behaviour require Founder acceptance.
